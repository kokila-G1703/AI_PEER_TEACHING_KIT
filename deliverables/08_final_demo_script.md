# Final Demo Script (3–5 minutes)

1. **Problem:** Students often find some topics difficult to explain to classmates.
2. **Solution:** PeerTeach AI creates a topic-specific teaching kit from the student's own topic.
3. **Live demo:** Enter a topic not shown in the example chips, choose a level and language, then generate the kit.
4. **Show outputs:** Explanation, misconceptions, 10-minute lesson plan, six slide outlines, handout, and five-question quiz.
5. **Quiz:** Submit the quiz and show the score history page.
6. **Verification:** Explain that AI may hallucinate; show the verification report and one real correction, if one was found.
7. **Ethics:** Discuss privacy, copyright, AI disclosure and free-tier API limits.
8. **Evaluation:** Present real pre/post scores and learner feedback. Clearly state the sample size and limitations.
9. **Conclusion:** The app supports peer teaching; it does not replace teacher judgment or independent fact-checking.
