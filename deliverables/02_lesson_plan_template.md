# 10-Minute Peer Lesson Plan

**Topic:** [Student-selected topic]  
**Audience:** [Class / small group]  
**Learning objective:** By the end, learners can explain the main idea and answer at least 4 of 5 quiz questions.

| Time | Activity | Presenter action | Learner action |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–1 min | Warm-up | Ask what learners already know | Share one idea |
| 1–3 min | Explain | Explain the core concept in simple words | Listen and ask questions |
| 3–5 min | Example | Walk through a practical example | Predict the next step |
| 5–7 min | Activity | Give a short pair/group task | Try the task |
| 7–9 min | Teach-back | Ask a learner to explain the idea | Explain in their own words |
| 9–10 min | Exit quiz | Give five quick questions or selected questions | Submit answers |

**After class:** Compare pre-test and post-test scores; record feedback and corrections.
